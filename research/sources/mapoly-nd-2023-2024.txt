P.M.B. 2210, ABEOKUTA
SCREENING EXERCISE FOR FULL - TIME NATIONAL DIPLOMA
(2023/2024 ACADEMIC SESSION)
All interested applicants, who made Moshood Abiola Polytechnic, Abeokuta their first and or second choice(s) of Institution(s) in 
the 2023/2024 UTME and others who did not choose Moshood Abiola Polytechnic, Abeokuta at all, but are eligible to study in 
Moshood Abiola Polytechnic, Abeokuta should note the following:
A. ELIGIBILITY
1. Candidates who chose Moshood Abiola Polytechnic, Abeokuta as their first or second choice(s) of Institution(s) during 
the 2023 UTME and scored 100 and above.
2. Candidates who did not choose Moshood Abiola Polytechnic, Abeokuta as either their first and/or second choice but 
who scored 100 and above in the 2023 UTME.
3. Candidates are to access the Polytechnic Website (my.mapoly.edu.ng) with the payment of Two Thousand Naira 
(N2,000.00) only through a Debit card from any bank of their choice(s).
4. Candidates will be expected to complete the screening form on-line.

C. METHOD OF APPLICATION
Candidates seeking admission into Moshood Abiola Polytechnic, Abeokuta are required to complete the Institution’s 
application form available on-line at my.mapoly.edu.ng with effect from the date of this publication, Monday, 17th July, 2023.
D. INSTRUCTION TO CANDIDATES
1. Candidates are to access the Website with Cash/Debit ATM card to the tune of Two Thousand Naira (N2,000.00) only at 
any bank of choice.
2. Log on to my.mapoly.edu.ng
3. Click on “Prospective Students”
4. Complete the application form as appropriate, upload your passport photograph, and supply other information (e-mail 
address(es), phone number(s) etc.)
5. Click on ‘submit’ to print your application form and screening slip.
E. SUBMISSION OF FORMS ONLINE
Candidates are hereby informed that submission of the applications for the screening on the Polytechnic’s website will close 
on Monday, 6th November, 2023.
F. RESULTS OF SCREENING EXERCISE
Results of the screening exercise will be published on the Polytechnic’s website.
